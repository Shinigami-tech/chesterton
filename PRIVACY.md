# Chesterton: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Chesterton is a Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian").

- **What it reads.** When you open the Chesterton panel or ask the Chesterton agent, the app reads the issue, its comments and links, linked issues, and Confluence search results for that issue key. It does this through Atlassian's APIs, using the app's permissions on your site.
- **What it writes.** Chesterton creates a Jira comment only when a user clicks "Pin this fence" or asks the agent to pin. It creates the demo project only when an admin clicks "Create demo data".
- **What it stores.** Nothing. Chesterton keeps no database, cache or logs of your content outside the request, and it sends no data to external services or domains.
- **Personal data.** Display names of people involved in an issue are shown to users who already have permission to see that issue. They are not stored or shared.
- **Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/Shinigami-tech/chesterton/issues.
